DECISION
  Keeping 95% of the variance needs 34 components instead of 48 features (29% fewer columns).
  The variance is spread over many components (no dominant one): the features are mostly not
  redundant, which agrees with the correlation matrix (Fig 7) and the feature selection step.
  We KEEP THE ORIGINAL FEATURES for the midterm dataset because:
    1. interpretability matters for a health question (an odds ratio for 'COPD' is meaningful,
       a weight on 'PC4' is not);
    2. the compression gain is moderate and would mix personal and regional information;
    3. most columns are 0/1 indicators, for which PCA (built for continuous data) is less natural.
  The PCA-transformed version is kept as an option to compare in the future modelling phase
  (e.g. for distance-based models such as k-NN or clustering, which suffer most from many dimensions).

Fig 15 reading: mean PC1 score = +2.89 for lung cancer cases vs -0.01 for the other adults.
PC1 is the 'age and chronic-disease burden' axis (see loadings), so cases sit on its high side,
but the two groups overlap heavily - two components are not enough to separate a rare outcome.
