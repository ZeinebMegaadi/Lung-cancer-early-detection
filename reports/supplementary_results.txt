  pm25_mean              sum of squared rank differences = 10 -> rho = 1 - 6 x 10 / (4 x 15) = 0.0
  smoking_rate           sum of squared rank differences = 10 -> rho = 1 - 6 x 10 / (4 x 15) = 0.0
  lung_cancer_incidence  sum of squared rank differences =  0 -> rho = 1 - 6 x 0 / (4 x 15) = 1.0
With n = 4, Spearman rho can only take the values 0, +-0.2, +-0.4, ..., +-1, so a between-region rho of
exactly 0 is a real value (not a computation error); with 4 points it is not evidence either way.
Reading: the registry agreement comes from differences BETWEEN regions (the West is low on both measures);
year-to-year changes within a region do not move together. With effectively 4 independent regions, the
agreement is descriptive support for the target, not a formal validation, and its p-value is optimistic.
